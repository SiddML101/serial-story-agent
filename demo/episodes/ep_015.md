Marlo grabs Leo’s jacket collar, hauling him back two inches. "Don't move, Vance," Marlo whispers, his breathing shallow and rapid against Leo's ear. "That's not an incoming parcel."

"Someone dragged something behind the timber," Leo says, keeping his light fixed on the dark shape slumped in the alcove.

"Or someone crawled back there to die," Marlo replies, his voice tight with an edge of quiet horror. "Keep your hands off the frame."

Leo sidesteps a pile of rotting pine slats, his boot kicking a discarded cast-iron boiler inspection plug wedged into a crushed sack of dried mortar. The heavy metal clinks off the cellar floor, but no sound comes from the shadow in the alcove.

Leo edges closer, tilting the flashlight beam lower. The dark bundle isn't cargo. It is a man, propped upright against the raw stone foundation, dressed in the dark wool uniform coat of a FlashDrop courier. White salt-crust lines the hem of his trousers, and his chin rests heavy against his chest, exposing a tarnished enamel lapel pin reading *COURIER 4409*.

Marlo steps beside Leo, his beam hitting the dead man’s sunken face. "Kallen," Marlo says. The word comes out flat, ruined by sudden realization. "Gideon told the whole floor he was transferred to Apartment 4B while he was still breathing. He’s been rotting down here in the dark."

"He never made it out of the depot," Leo says.

The air in the alcove smells of cold copper and damp earth. Kallen’s skin has turned the color of dry tallow, pulled tight across his cheekbones, his jaws set in a hard lock. His left leg is bent awkwardly under the bottom shelf of Cage Seven, but his right arm rests stiffly across his lap, his blue-tinted fingers frozen in a rigid fist.

Leo crouches beside the body. "Look at his hand."

"Don't touch him, Leo," Marlo warns, grabbing Leo's shoulder. "If you disturb the scene, Gideon will trigger the terminal lock from upstairs."

"Gideon already knows we're here," Leo says. He reaches down and carefully works his fingers between Kallen's stiff, icy thumb and palm.

Locked inside Kallen's grip is a heavy silver pocket watch with a spiderweb crack across its glass face. Leo pulls it free from the corpse's frozen fingers. The polished silver casing is cold against Leo's skin, smeared with dark soil.

Through the fractured crystal, the internal balance wheel sits completely still. The fine black steel hands point frozen and immovable to 3:14 AM.
