# The Afterlife Manifest: 200-episode arc plan

**Premise:** A delivery rider realizes every address on today's route belongs to someone who died in the same building.  
**Logline:** A exhausted bicycle courier discovers his daily delivery route consists entirely of apartments where former tenants died under mysterious circumstances, forcing him into a web of urban horror and corporate necromancy.  
**Plan version:** v11

## Story bible

Title: The Afterlife Manifest
Logline: A exhausted bicycle courier discovers his daily delivery route consists entirely of apartments where former tenants died under mysterious circumstances, forcing him into a web of urban horror and corporate necromancy.
Genre: Urban Gothic Mystery / Thriller | Tone: Gritty, noirish, paranoid, and claustrophobic
POV: close third person on Leo Vance | Tense: present
Setting: Oakhaven City, a rain-soaked industrial metropolis centered around the Ashwood Monolith—a crumbling 40-story residential complex built in 1978.
World rules:
- The packages delivered to deceased tenants contain physical echoes or unresolved earthly items from the moment of their death.
- The Ashwood Monolith traps the spiritual residue of anyone who dies inside its walls, preventing them from crossing over.
- Dispatch assignments cannot be refused or altered once uploaded to a courier's terminal without physical harm occurring to the rider.
- No supernatural entity can directly physically strike a living person; they can only manipulate electronics, physical objects, and psychological perception.
- The courier route algorithm is entirely automated and runs on a closed analog network beneath the city mortuary.
Style guide:
- Focus on tangible sensory details: rust, slick asphalt, ozone smells, grease, and metallic hums.
- Keep sentences punchy and tense during delivery runs; stretch syntax into deliberate horror during apartment entries.
- Maintain strict third-person limited perspective centered on Leo's physical fatigue and observations.
- Avoid romanticizing the supernatural; present horrific occurrences as cold administrative processes.
- End daily episodes on active cliffhangers or immediate tactile discoveries.

## Main cast

- **Leo Vance** (`leo`, protagonist): A twenty-eight-year-old night courier for FlashDrop Logistics with worn boots, chronic sleep deprivation, and a strict work ethic. *Goal:* Survive his shift, clear his debts, and uncover why he is receiving dead people's delivery orders. *Secret:* He survived an apartment fire as a child in the Ashwood Monolith that killed his mother.
- **Mira Chen** (`mira`, ally): An archivist and former tenant who operates a small print shop near the Ashwood Monolith. *Goal:* Expose the illegal tenant mortality cover-ups orchestrated by the building owners. *Secret:* Her older brother died inside Apartment 1204, but his death was reported as an out-of-state disappearance.
- **Gideon Vance-Cross** (`gideon`, antagonist): The cold, immaculate dispatch manager at FlashDrop Logistics who oversees high-value routes. *Goal:* Ensure the supernatural delivery cycle continues smoothly to honor corporate contracts. *Secret:* He receives personal financial kickbacks for every deceased tenant whose estate remains unclosed.
- **Marlo Briggs** (`marlo`, ally): A veteran fifty-year-old courier who rides a battered steel-frame fixed-gear bike. *Goal:* Earn enough money to abandon the city and leave his past behind. *Secret:* He carried the first supernatural manifest fifteen years ago and sacrificed a fellow rider to escape.
- **Elena Rostova** (`elena`, ally): A mysterious tenant living illegally on the condemned 13th floor of the Ashwood Monolith. *Goal:* Find her missing sister's personal diary trapped in an unassigned delivery package. *Secret:* She has been living inside the building illegally for three years using dead tenants' utility accounts.
- **Arthur Vane** (`vane`, antagonist): The reclusive, elderly owner of the Ashwood Monolith and founder of Vane Real Estate Holdings. *Goal:* Maintain the ritual containment field in the building to prolong his own life. *Secret:* He uses the life residue of deceased tenants to slow his biological aging.

## Character arcs

- **leo**: Debt-ridden, passive courier -> Courageous whistleblower who dismantles a corporate necromancy syndicate
- **mira**: Isolated, fearful archivist -> Confident investigative journalist leading public exposure
- **gideon**: Smug, controlling dispatch director -> Desperate, disgraced criminal facing total exposure
- **marlo**: Guilt-ridden, cynical veteran -> Heroic mentor who achieves redemption through sacrifice
- **elena**: Wild, secretive survivalist -> Connected advocate for building survivors
- **vane**: Unreachable, immortal landlord -> Fragile, exposed villain defeated by his own greed

## Acts

- **Act 1: The Dead Manifest** (eps 1-40). Establish the terrifying daily routine, reveal that every package address belongs to a dead tenant, and introduce the corrupt network surrounding FlashDrop. *Turning point:* Leo opens a return manifest and finds an official death certificate bearing his own mother's name and the Ashwood Monolith address.
- **Act 2: Corridors of Ashwood** (eps 41-80). Infiltrate the internal structure of the Ashwood Monolith, map its hidden spaces, and discover how the packages are generated. *Turning point:* Marlo, Leo's mentor, betrays Leo by stealing the basement vault key and turning him in to Gideon to pay off his own debt.
- **Act 3: Unlawful Possession** (eps 81-120). Uncover the corporate connection between FlashDrop Logistics and Arthur Vane's life-extension apparatus. *Turning point:* Vane's enforcers firebomb Mira's print shop archive, destroying critical paper records and leaving Mira critically injured.
- **Act 4: The Terminal Breach** (eps 121-160). Turn the tables on the dispatch network by seizing control of the dispatch terminal and turning the algorithm against Vane. *Turning point:* Leo successfully hacks the central dispatch node, reversing the routing flow and threatening to collapse Vane's containment system.
- **Act 5: Final Delivery** (eps 161-200). Confront Arthur Vane in the penthouse, dismantle the automated death system, and free the trapped souls of the Ashwood Monolith. *Turning point:* Leo broadcast-streams the live confession of Arthur Vane while destroying the master server as the Monolith's sub-basement implodes.
